# Gabrielvision Song Contest

A karaoke singing game in the browser: sing into your mic, a comet traces your pitch over scrolling
note bars, and you score more the closer you stay to each note. A speech model running in your
browser checks that you sing the words. Up to two singers, three difficulties, and a vocal coach report.

Play: https://gabrielpetersson.github.io/gabrielvision/ (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari; headphones recommended)

Songs are public-domain compositions (Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star; Happy Birthday to You; Amazing Grace)
in new arrangements made for this game. Instruments use the GeneralUser GS soundfont. Lyric check:
Whisper base.en via transformers.js. Your voice never leaves your device.
